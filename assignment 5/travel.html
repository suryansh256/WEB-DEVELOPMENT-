Welcome to Kashmir

Kashmir is known for its beautiful mountains, lakes, gardens and valleys. It is a wonderful destination for travellers who love nature and adventure.

Destination Details

Best Time to Visit

March to October for greenery and pleasant weather. December to February for snow experiences.

Popular Attractions

  • Dal Lake
  • Gulmarg
  • Pahalgam
  • Sonamarg
  • Mughal Gardens

Estimated Budget

₹10,000 to ₹25,000 per person for a short trip, depending on accommodation and travel.

Travel Tips

  • Carry warm clothes.
  • Book accommodation in advance.
  • Check weather conditions before travelling.
  • Keep identification documents with you.
